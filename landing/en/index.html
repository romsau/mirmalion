French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese.

Dictation

You speak, the text lands right where your cursor is.

One shortcut, ⌃⌥, and you dictate into any application. The text is inserted exactly where you were, punctuated and clean.

A second shortcut, ⌃⌥⌘, writes straight into the language of your choice. A personal dictionary learns your names and your trade's vocabulary.

Live

A conference in another language, readable live.

You're following a conference in German, a video call in Spanish, a video in Italian: Mirmalion captures the sound on your Mac and displays the text as it goes, translated into your language if you want.

At the end, a summary written on your machine by a local language model, and an export of the text.

Files

A recording, dropped, transcribed.

Drop an audio or video file and get its text back. No link to paste, no upload: the file never leaves your disk.

Nothing leaves your machine.

Every feature works without a network. No telemetry, no account, no content sent anywhere. The network is only used for two explicit actions: downloading a language, and updating the app.
